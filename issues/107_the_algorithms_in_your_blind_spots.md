# The Algorithms in Your Blind Spots

###### First posted on Jun 22, 2025

One of my smartest friends was using a radio antenna to try to identify a ship’s transmitter signal. After a month of trying, he failed to isolate the transmitter’s signal from the noisy radio spectrum.

Why did he fail?

It’s because he was using dynamic programming (a computer science algorithm) when he needed to use a machine learning approach.

I can’t blame him - because he was TRAINED to be an excellent computer scientist. But he had no experience with machine learning - so he didn’t reach for those approaches.

Often, the approach one first picks is from their area of training, but it may not be the best one. Even if your confidence is high, your posterior probability is biased since you are blind to more appropriate techniques.

I think the solution is to make sure that at least one other friend (with a substantially different background than you) understands the problem you’re solving (to the point where they can explain it to someone else) so that they can easily point out solutions you have missed.

After all, it’s impossible to find a single person excellent in ML, CS, and optimization.

\- Curtis
